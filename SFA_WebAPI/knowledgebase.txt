- 🟣 Full Member

**How to Choose a Group:**
- If unsure, try a level just below what you think you can do, or join a Try-Out ride first.
- You can move between groups as your fitness or preferences change.
- Contact clubruns@sanfairyanncc.co.uk for advice.

**Group List & Typical Details:**

- **A Group**: 60+ miles, 19+ mph. Coordinator: Mark Cain. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/agroup)
- **Fast Inters**: 50 miles, 16–19 mph. Coordinator: Dudley Curtis. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/fast-inters)
- **Inters Plus**: 50–70 miles, 15–17 mph. Coordinator: Vacant. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/inters-plus)
- **Saturday 9am Inters**: 45–65 miles, 14–17 mph. Coordinator: Paul Ward. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/saturday9aminters)
- **Saturday 10am Inters**: 45–60 miles, 14–16 mph. Coordinator: Vacant. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/saturday-10am-inters)
- **Sunday Intermediates**: 50–65 miles, 13–16 mph. Coordinator: Eric Watts. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/sunday-intermediates)
- **Inbetweeners**: 40–55 miles, 13–15 mph. Coordinator: Patrick O’Donnell-Bourke. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/inbetweeners)
- **B+ Group**: 35–50 miles, 13–15 mph. Coordinator: Tony Palmer. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/b-group)
- **B Group**: 30–40 miles, 12–14 mph. Coordinator: Philip Thorpe. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/b-group-1)
- **C Group**: 25–40 miles, 10–12 mph. Coordinator: Sandy Ray. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/c-group)
- **Midweekers**: 30–45 miles, 11–16 mph. Coordinator: Russell Chidwick. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/midweekers)
- **D+ Group**: 30–40 miles, 12–14 mph. Coordinator: Richard Valentine. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/d-group)
- **D Group**: 25–35 miles, approx. 11–13 mph. Coordinator: Richard Valentine. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/d-group-1)
- **EasyRiders+**: 25–30 miles, approx. 10–12 mph. Coordinators: Gail & Nick Crutchfield. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/easyriders-1)
- **Mountain Bike Group**: Speed/distance varies. Coordinator: Martyn Peal. [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/mountain-bike-group)
- **Virtual Riding**: About 1 hour, all speeds. Coordinator: Jonathan Leeson (Zwift). [More info](https://www.sanfairyanncc.co.uk/virtual-riding)

**General Advice:**
- All group rides are social, not competitive.
- You can try any club ride for free twice before joining.
- Under 18s must bring a completed Parent/Guardian Consent Form and ICE details.
- Always check with your group for any changes due to weather or other factors.
